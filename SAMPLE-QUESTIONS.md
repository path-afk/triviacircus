# Sample Analyst Trivia Questions — Jay Draft

## Geopolitics & International Relations
1. Which country currently holds the largest non-US stock of US Treasury securities?
   → Japan (~$1.1T as of 2024; China dropped below Japan in 2022)
   
2. The Thucydides Trap describes the tendency for conflict when a rising power threatens an established one. Name the historian who coined the modern term.
   → Graham Allison (Harvard, 2012)

3. Which international body holds legal jurisdiction over maritime disputes in the South China Sea under UNCLOS?
   → The Permanent Court of Arbitration — China rejected its 2016 ruling

## Markets, Finance & Economics
4. What was the primary mechanism that caused the 2008 financial crisis to spread from US subprime mortgages to global bank balance sheets?
   → CDO tranching and CDS exposure — banks held AAA-rated tranches backed by subprime MBS

5. Which central bank was the first to introduce a negative interest rate policy?
   → Sweden's Riksbank (July 2009, briefly; then sustained from 2015)

6. Name the economic theory that argues trade deficits are irrelevant to employment because they reflect capital account surpluses.
   → Mundell-Fleming / Sectoral Balances — associated with Warren Mosler and MMT, also standard macro

## Technology & Engineering  
7. Rust guarantees memory safety without a garbage collector through what compile-time system?
   → The ownership and borrow checker system

8. What does a Byzantine fault-tolerant system protect against that a crash-fault-tolerant system does not?
   → Arbitrary/malicious behavior by nodes, not just silent failure

9. The CAP theorem states a distributed system cannot simultaneously guarantee all three of: Consistency, Availability, and what?
   → Partition tolerance

## Science & Nature
10. What is the name of the protein whose misfolding causes Parkinson's disease to propagate between neurons?
    → Alpha-synuclein

11. Why does the James Webb Space Telescope observe primarily in infrared rather than visible light?
    → Redshift of distant objects + infrared penetrates dust; also mirrors optimized for IR

## History (Causation Focus)
12. The 1973 oil embargo was triggered by US support for which country during which war?
    → Israel during the Yom Kippur War (October War)

13. What single policy decision by the Allies after WWI is most cited by historians as enabling the rise of fascism in Germany?
    → The Versailles reparations structure + war guilt clause (Article 231)

## Culture & Ideas
14. Which philosopher argued that "the unexamined life is not worth living" — and was sentenced to death partly for it?
    → Socrates

15. Name the 1972 paper that introduced the concept of "information asymmetry" in markets, later winning a Nobel Prize.
    → "The Market for Lemons" by George Akerlof (Nobel 2001)
